## Project Showcase

### Wine Quality Analysis: What Factors Affect Red Wine Quality?

**Research Question:**  
Which physicochemical properties most influence the quality rating of red wine?

**Visualization:**  
![Correlation Heatmap](images/fig1_correlation_heatmap.png)

**Brief Explanation of Findings:**  
The correlation heatmap shows that alcohol content is positively correlated with quality rating, while volatile acidity is negatively correlated. Citric acid and sulphates also show weak positive correlations. The main finding is that red wines with higher alcohol and lower volatile acidity tend to receive higher quality ratings.
